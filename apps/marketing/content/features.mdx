# Core Platform Features

LeadForge OS provides a suite of local prospecting engines to automate business discovery, lead verification, and campaign delivery.

## Business Discovery

Search and extract listings using built-in maps crawlers:
- **Sidebar Parsing**: Automatically search target regions and parse business sidebar elements.
- **Redirect Resolution**: Resolve website redirects to find canonical root domains.
- **Listing Data Extraction**: Harvest business names, addresses, phone numbers, ratings, and social URLs.

## Deep Web Crawling

Analyze discovered websites using depth-controlled crawlers:
- **Contact Harvesting**: Scan public pages to extract emails, phone numbers, and social media handles.
- **Spam Filtering**: Automatically ignore tracking pixels, tracking links, and false contact scripts.
- **Compliance Rules**: Obeys standard exclusion guidelines to scrape targets responsibly.

## Social Enrichment

Enrich lead lists with key executive profiles:
- **Decision Maker Target**: Query profiles to identify CEOs, Founders, and Sales Directors matching your target industry.
- **Fallbacks**: Utilize alternative search queries if direct cookies are not active.

## Outreach Sequences

Draft and coordinate personalized outreach sequences:
- **Dynamic Variable Injection**: Inject fields (e.g., recipient name, business type, local city) into email templates.
- **SMTP Send Scheduler**: Limit send rates to protect IP reputation.
- **IMAP Reply Tracking**: Listen to incoming replies, correlate them with sent outreach, and automatically pause sequences for replied contacts.
